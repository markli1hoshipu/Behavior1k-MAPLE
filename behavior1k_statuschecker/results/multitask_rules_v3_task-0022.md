1. move to - Both grippers clamp shoes AND the white rack/shelving is large, near and centred, filling much of the frame with its shelves readable and the robot already standing at it (even if other shoes already sit on its shelves); also use this if no shoe of any kind appears anywhere in the frame. A rack that is small, far down the hallway, or only a sliver at the frame edge does NOT qualify.

2. pick up from - Any loose shoe lies on the bare wooden floor anywhere in view (near or several meters off), OR the grippers hold shoes while the view is still open floor / hallway / doorway / mirror with the rack absent, distant, or only at the frame periphery. Grippers may be empty, reaching down, or already clamped — holding shoes is not evidence of "move to"; carrying shoes over plain floor is still this label. When torn between this and "move to" while shoes are held, choose this.

3. place in - Both grippers are empty (open, lowered, nothing clamped) and the white rack is in view with shoes resting on its shelves. Empty grippers plus rack is decisive and outranks "move to". If any shoe is still clamped, it is not this label. A just-released frame and an approach frame differ only in gripper contents — rely on that alone, never on distance to the rack.
