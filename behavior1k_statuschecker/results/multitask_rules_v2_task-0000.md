1. move to - No robot arm, forearm or gripper anywhere in the frame AND you are still far from the coffee table: the ceiling, the hanging lamp and the framed picture above the sofa are all visible and the radio is only a tiny red speck. Use only for clearly distant views.
2. pick up from - The default label, covering two looks: (a) no arms visible but you are already close, so the grey table top fills the lower half, the sofa fills the upper half, and little ceiling or wall art shows; (b) a black-and-white arm reaches in from the right and its gripper is at or holding the radio while the radio is a narrow upright red slab with white stripes and NO round dial facing you.
3. press - The radio lies flat with its wide red top face and its large round black dial clearly facing the camera, and a gripper is touching that top face. No visible dial means it is not press.
4. place on - The radio rests flat on the grey table with both grippers empty and pulled back to the frame edges. A gripper still holding the upright radio near the table is indistinguishable from (b) in one frame - lifting vs lowering is motion, not appearance - so answer pick up from unless the radio is clearly already released.
