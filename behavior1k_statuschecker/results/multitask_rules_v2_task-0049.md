1. chop - Knife clearly gripped, blade over the board; a bare board with pieces on it is not chop.
2. close door - Fridge/oven standing open, interior shelves filling the view, arm near the door edge; identical to "open door" in a still frame — motion direction is unrecoverable.
3. move to - Distant wide view of a kitchen corner or an appliance several feet off, arms idle at the frame edge, nothing within gripper reach; prefer this over "place on" whenever nothing is being touched.
4. open door - Fridge/oven fills the view close up and a gripper reaches for its handle; the door may still be shut. Not separable from "close door".
5. pick up from - Empty open gripper reaching down at an object still resting on a counter, board, or fridge shelf. This is the default when a gripper hovers near an object.
6. place in - Item still gripped, carried low into the oven cavity or fridge interior.
7. place on - Only when an open gripper withdraws above an item already sitting flat; not merely because dishes are on a counter.
8. place on next to - Indistinguishable from "place on" in one frame; the "next to" relation is not visible.
9. pour - Gripped board, tub, or bowl tilted above a plate or sink, contents spilling out.
10. pull tray - Oven open, wire rack/tray visible, gripper on the rack edge.
11. push to - Gripper pressed flat on a board or dish sliding it along the counter; close, blurred, object never lifted.
