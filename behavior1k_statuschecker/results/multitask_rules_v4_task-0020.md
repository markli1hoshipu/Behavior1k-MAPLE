1. move to - No object is held and the hands are either out of frame or empty, with the camera looking across the room at a counter, bowl, or basket that is still some distance away.
2. pick up from - A gripper is reaching down at, into, or alongside a basket that is still RESTING on a counter or table (the basket is supported by the surface, not lifted); this counts even if the basket is small in the view, is seen from above at an angle, or looks empty because the vegetables inside are hidden by the rim, the gripper, or glare — reaching toward a resting basket is "pick up from" regardless of whether any vegetable is visible. Frames with no hand visible at all cannot be told from "move to" in a still image.
3. place in - A red bowl is close and clearly in view with a hand right at its rim, usually with a vegetable at the hand or already sitting in the bowl.
4. place on - A hand is gripping and carrying an empty basket that is clearly LIFTED clear of the surface (raised, tilted, or held out in front of the body with a gap under it), close to the camera and hovering just over a counter surface; if the basket is still sitting flat on the surface, this is not "place on".
5. Note - "place in" and "place on" cannot be told apart by the moment of release; use the held object instead (vegetable going toward a red bowl = place in, empty basket over a counter = place on). Likewise, a hand touching a full basket looks the same whether the robot is starting to pick up or has just finished, so early "pick up from" and late "move to" frames are not separable from one still image.
