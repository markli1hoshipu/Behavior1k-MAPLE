1. move to - The robot is walking/turning with the brown-and-white bin carried upright (mouth up) in the left gripper while the right arm hangs back at the lower-right frame edge, not extended into the scene; the upper half of the frame shows distant room, furniture, or doorway, and any soda can is small and far off. This is the default when nothing is being grasped or reached for.
2. pick up from - The right arm is extended forward and down into the scene toward the floor, fingers open above or closed around a soda can within about one bin-width of the gripper, and/or the carried bin is tilted with its mouth swung down toward a nearby can; the floor fills most of the frame. When the arm is not reaching and no can is at the gripper, this is indistinguishable from "move to" in a still frame - default to move to.
3. place in - A soda can is visibly gripped by a gripper while the open mouth of the bin appears in the same frame; the can and bin need not be touching yet.
4. place on - The bin is standing on the floor with soda cans visible inside it, and both grippers are open, empty, and drawn back on either side of it - nothing is held. Applies even when the room and doorway fill most of the frame; a floor-resting can-filled bin with empty hands outranks "move to".
