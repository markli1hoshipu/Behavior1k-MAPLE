1. move to - Wide view down the kitchen: no basket or bowl within arm's reach in the lower frame; grippers empty, at the frame edge, or out of view; also use this when a gripper carries a loose vegetable through open space, even toward a red bowl.

2. pick up from - Default whenever both grippers reach down onto a wicker basket that sits on the near white counter and fills the lower-center of the frame, whether that basket holds vegetables or looks empty. A small red bowl on a far counter is background decor, not evidence — ignore it.

3. place in - Only when a red bowl is large and close, filling a substantial part of the frame, with a gripper right at its rim or a vegetable resting inside it. If the red bowl is small and far away, never choose this.

4. place on - A gripper holds a wicker basket lifted clear of the counter, tilted in mid-air, with no large red bowl nearby.

Limits that wording cannot fix: a gripper closed on a basket is identical at grasp-start and release-end, so pick up from vs. place on vs. the surrounding move to frames are not separable from one still image; likewise a vegetable in transit toward a bowl and the moment of release into it look the same.
