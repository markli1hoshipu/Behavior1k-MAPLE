1. move to - Neither gripper grasps anything: hands empty, at the frame edge, or out of view, with a wide shot of the room (floor, far cabinets) and any counter, bowl or basket still at a distance. Also use it when a vegetable already lies released in a red bowl and the camera has pulled back.
2. pick up from - Default whenever a gripper is at a woven basket: open around its rim, closed on it, or carrying it, whether or not vegetables are still inside. Prefer this over "place on" for every basket-in-hand frame.
3. place in - A red bowl fills a close counter-corner view, often cut off at the frame edge, and a vegetable is still clamped in the gripper at or just above its rim.
4. place on - Rare; only an emptied basket held above a bare stretch of counter, nothing inside it and no red bowl nearby. Otherwise do not pick this label.
5. Note - Basket contact looks identical whether grasping, carrying, or releasing, so "place on" is not separable from "pick up from" in a still frame; default to "pick up from". A vegetable sitting in a bowl likewise cannot separate a finished "place in" from the start of the next "move to"; use camera distance (close = place in, pulled back = move to). If no hand is visible, default to "move to".
