1. chop - Gripper clenched on the knife handle, blade tilted down onto the board with cube pieces. A knife lying flat beside the gripper is never chop.

2. close door - Appliance interior already visible; gripper out at the door edge, not reaching inside, usually already holding a retrieved item.

3. move to - Pulled-back travelling view: floor and far walls fill the frame, counters distant, no gripper touching anything (may carry a tub, but not lowering it).

4. open door - Appliance front still SHUT, no shelves or rack visible, gripper reaching toward it or at the handle. A closed door means open door.

5. pick up from - One gripper closed on an object held clear of the surface: board raised in air, hand among open fridge shelves.

6. place in - Both grippers hold food low over the open oven cavity, lowering it in.

7. place on - Object rests on counter, board or sink basin, gripper loose beside it, camera close overhead; prefer over pick up from when both arms frame it.

8. place on next to - Like place on, but on a bare counter stretch with no board, tray or sink beneath; otherwise indistinguishable from place on.

9. pour - Held board or tub tipped over a plate or basin, pieces coming out.

10. pull tray - Oven open, wire rack visible in the cavity, gripper at the oven mouth.

11. push to - Motion-blurred close view, gripper pressed flat against a board or dish that stays on the counter, no knife held.
