1. move to - Both grippers are raised holding shoes AND the white shoe rack / shelving unit is already visible ahead or beside the robot (the approach-with-cargo phase); also use this if no shoe appears anywhere in the frame at all.

2. pick up from - A shoe lies on the bare wooden floor anywhere in view (near or several meters ahead) with no rack dominating the scene, OR the grippers are holding shoes while the view is still the open floor/hallway/doorway where the shoes were lying and the rack is absent or only a sliver at the frame edge. Grippers may be empty, reaching down, or already clamped on a shoe — a held shoe over plain floor is still this label.

3. place in - Shoes are resting inside or on the shelves of the white rack and the grippers are empty (open, lowered, nothing clamped). Empty grippers plus shoes on the rack is the decisive signature; if shoes are still clamped in the grippers, it is not this label.

Note: a held shoe with the rack in view genuinely cannot be split from "place in" by appearance alone — release state (empty gripper) is the only static evidence, so rely on it rather than distance to the rack.
