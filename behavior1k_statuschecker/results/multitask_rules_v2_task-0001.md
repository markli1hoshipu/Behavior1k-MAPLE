1. move to - Robot's left gripper carries the tan/brown bucket at the very bottom edge while the right gripper is empty; the upper half of the frame shows the room ahead (walls, doorway, sofa, stairs, horizon). No can is held in any gripper — distant cans on the floor are tiny specks. Default here whenever the only object near the grippers is the empty-looking bucket.

2. pick up from - Camera tilted down so floor fills the frame, and one gripper is closed on or descending onto a soda can at floor level, clearly below and away from the bucket's rim.

3. place in - Both grippers converge together over the bucket's open mouth with a can gripped between them at rim height, cans visible inside. Limit: a single frame showing a held can beside the bucket cannot separate "just grabbed" from "about to drop" — that depends on motion.

4. place on - The bucket stands unheld on the floor with cans inside it, and both grippers are open, empty and spread wide apart, touching nothing. Grippers empty is the decisive cue, not the room content.
