1. move to - No arms anywhere in frame and the camera is still far back: the whole room is in view, including the right-hand wall shelf with small picture frames, and the red radio is a tiny distant sliver. Use only for these widest shots.
2. pick up from - The default label here. Either no arms yet but the camera has closed in (sofa fills the upper frame, little ceiling, shelf photos gone, radio noticeably bigger), or arms have entered and the radio is a narrow upright red-and-white striped slab held high, lifted clear of the tabletop so it overlaps the wood floor or sofa behind it, with the left arm only a short stub in the bottom-left corner. The armless approach frames cannot be separated from "move to" in a still frame; default to pick up from, which is far more common.
3. press - The radio lies flat with its wide rounded red top face and large round dark dial facing the camera (a small green light once on), and a gripper is down on that face. If that dial face is not visible, it is not press.
4. place on - The radio is back down low on the grey tabletop beside the glowing round lamp, edge-on and striped again, while the left arm reaches in horizontally from the left edge at mid-height, elbow and open gripper visible; last frames show it standing free with both grippers withdrawn.
