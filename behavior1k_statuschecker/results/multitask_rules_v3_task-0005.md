1. move to - A wide view with a broad stretch of floor (often ceiling or doorway too) between the robot and a small, distant dresser/sink/toilet; arms are either absent from the frame entirely or hang low at the sides, sometimes already carrying traps. If the dresser is visible but no arm or gripper appears anywhere in the frame, it is always move to, never pick up from.

2. pick up from - The wooden dresser with the framed picture fills the lower frame at arm's length and at least one black-and-white gripper is in view, down at or just in front of the dresser surface among the small traps. The moment just after a grasp (gripper raised above the dresser holding a trap, dresser still close) is labelled move to but cannot be distinguished from pick up from in a still frame.

3. place on next to - The white sink or toilet is centred and close, the robot squared up to it with only a tile or two of floor showing, and both grippers hang at the bottom edge near the floor beside the fixture. Approach frames where the fixture is still several tiles away look identical to move to in a still image; when a gripper reaches toward the floor beside a bathroom fixture, prefer place on next to, the more common label here.
