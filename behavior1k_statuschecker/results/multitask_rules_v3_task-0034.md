1. hang - Both grippers are up at countertop/cabinet height and overlap the cabinets, so the pale floor is reduced to a narrow wedge trapped between the two arms rather than a band running beneath and beyond them; the tall garden window is cropped by the frame edge and no ceiling or track lights are visible. The framed poster may still look small and distant — judge by gripper height and how little floor remains, not by poster size. A pale object held at the left gripper at counter height is the carried poster, never a floor object.

2. move to - The window is cropped by the frame edge and the ceiling is out of frame (kitchen is close), but both grippers still point down low over a wide unbroken band of floor that reaches the cabinet bases, and the small pale object is already at or inside the left gripper's fingers with no gap. Low arms over open floor outrank how large the cabinets look.

3. pick up from - Wide framing: ceiling and track lights visible, the whole tall garden window uncropped, the doorway/adjacent room at left, and a large floor expanse. A small pale object lies on the floor ahead of the left gripper, separate from it — even a centimetres-wide gap counts. This combination is pick up from, never move to and never hang.
